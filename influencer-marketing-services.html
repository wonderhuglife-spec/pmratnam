Influencer Marketing FAQ

How do you choose the right influencers for my brand?
We vet creators based on audience demographics, past engagement rates, authenticity, and brand safety, ensuring their followers closely match your ideal customer profile.
Are micro-influencers better than mega-influencers?
Often, yes! Micro-influencers (10k-50k followers) typically boast significantly higher engagement rates and deeper trust with their community, resulting in higher conversion rates at a fraction of the cost.
