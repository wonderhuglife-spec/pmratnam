Digital Marketing FAQ

Should I invest in SEO, Paid Ads, or Social Media first?
It depends on your immediate business goals. If you need sales *now*, Paid Ads (PPC) are the best starting point. If you are building a long-term brand asset and want sustainable traffic, SEO is critical. A healthy 360° strategy uses PPC for short-term revenue while building SEO and Social Media for long-term dominance.
How do you track the success of a digital marketing campaign?
We use advanced tracking setups via Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4. We track specific "conversion actions" such as form submissions, phone calls, WhatsApp clicks, and eCommerce purchases. This allows us to calculate your exact Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) and Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).
